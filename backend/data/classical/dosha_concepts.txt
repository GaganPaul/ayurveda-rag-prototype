TITLE: Dosha Concepts in Ayurveda
TYPE: Traditional-knowledge educational summary

Ayurvedic theory describes three doshas: Vata, Pitta, and Kapha. They are used in Ayurvedic explanations of physiological tendencies, balance, imbalance, and constitution.

VATA
Traditional descriptions associate Vata with movement and functions involving motion, communication, and nervous-system-related activity. Traditional Ayurvedic descriptions commonly associate Vata with qualities such as mobility, lightness, and dryness.

PITTA
Traditional descriptions associate Pitta with transformation, digestion, metabolism, and heat. Traditional descriptions commonly associate Pitta with qualities such as heat, intensity, and fluidity.

KAPHA
Traditional descriptions associate Kapha with structure, stability, lubrication, and cohesion. Traditional descriptions commonly associate Kapha with qualities such as heaviness, stability, and moisture.

PRAKRITI
Prakriti is an Ayurvedic concept concerning an individual's constitutional tendency. Ayurvedic practitioners may use constitutional assessment as part of traditional practice.

EVIDENCE LIMITATION:
Doshas and Prakriti are concepts of the Ayurvedic system. They should not be described as scientifically established equivalents of specific hormones, organs, personality types, or medical diagnoses. A user's symptoms should not be used by this chatbot to assign a dosha or diagnose disease.

SAFE ANSWERING:
If asked "Which dosha do I have?", explain that determining an Ayurvedic constitution is traditionally done through a practitioner assessment and that an online chatbot cannot reliably diagnose or establish a medical condition. It may explain the traditional framework without presenting a personalized diagnosis.
