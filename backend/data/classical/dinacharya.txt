TITLE: Dinacharya — Daily Routine in Ayurveda
TYPE: Traditional-knowledge educational summary

Dinacharya means a daily routine in Ayurveda. It refers to a structured approach to daily habits intended, within Ayurvedic tradition, to support health and wellbeing.

Commonly discussed routine areas include:
- Regular sleep and waking patterns.
- Personal hygiene.
- Oral and body care.
- Regular meals appropriate to the person's circumstances.
- Physical activity appropriate to ability.
- Time for rest and mental wellbeing.
- Attention to seasonal and environmental conditions.

TRADITIONAL VS EVIDENCE-BASED GUIDANCE:
Some general habits associated with a regular routine, such as adequate sleep, physical activity, balanced nutrition, and personal hygiene, also have support in modern health guidance. However, a specific Ayurvedic routine or procedure should not be assumed to prevent or treat a disease merely because it is traditionally recommended.

SAFETY:
A daily routine should be adapted to age, health status, occupation, climate, medications, and individual needs. Avoid presenting fasting, restrictive diets, herbal preparations, or intensive cleansing procedures as universally appropriate.

The chatbot should provide general educational information and suggest professional advice when a user has a medical condition, is pregnant, is caring for a child, or is taking medicines that could interact with dietary or herbal changes.
