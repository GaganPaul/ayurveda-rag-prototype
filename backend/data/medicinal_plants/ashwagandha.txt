TITLE: Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)
TYPE: Botanical / traditional-use educational summary

Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) is a plant used in Ayurvedic traditions.

TRADITIONAL USE:
Ayurvedic traditions have used ashwagandha in preparations associated with general wellbeing and other traditional indications. Traditional use is not the same as proof of clinical effectiveness.

RESEARCH EVIDENCE:
Modern research has investigated standardized ashwagandha extracts for several outcomes, including stress-related measures, sleep, and exercise-related outcomes. Findings differ across studies and products, and evidence should be interpreted according to study design, sample size, preparation, dose, and duration.

SAFETY:
Herbal supplements can have adverse effects and can interact with medicines. Do not assume that "natural" means risk-free. People who are pregnant or breastfeeding, have significant liver or other medical conditions, or take prescription medicines should seek professional advice before using concentrated preparations.

CHATBOT RULE:
Never tell a user that ashwagandha definitely treats anxiety, insomnia, infertility, thyroid disease, cancer, or another medical condition. If the evidence is uncertain or condition-specific, say so.
