TITLE: Tulsi / Holy Basil (Ocimum species)
TYPE: Botanical / traditional-use educational summary

Tulsi, commonly called holy basil, refers to Ocimum plants used in Indian culinary and traditional contexts.

TRADITIONAL USE:
Tulsi has a long history of traditional use in South Asia. Traditional applications should be described as traditional uses rather than established medical treatments.

RESEARCH EVIDENCE:
Laboratory, animal, and human research has investigated different Ocimum preparations and outcomes. Evidence cannot be generalized across all extracts, teas, doses, or products.

SAFETY:
Different preparations may have different concentrations and constituents. People taking medicines or managing chronic conditions should consult a qualified healthcare professional before using concentrated herbal products.

CHATBOT RULE:
Do not claim tulsi prevents or cures infections, diabetes, cancer, respiratory disease, or other conditions without condition-specific high-quality evidence.
